Anatomic Buoyancies
_is a collection of work arranged from material collected during Justin Bennett's field-recording workshop in 2023(The Hague). The works seek to explore the characteristics of each microphone through the choice of environment2 they are placed inside. Each microphone embodies a unique shape and size, bandwidth, field of capture, and usability requirements that situates it in unique contexts. The (self-proclaimed) objective was to explore the characteristics of microphones presented to us, through imagining a suitable environment to capture. The following recordings were complied and composed during a week's excursion through the city, shifting sands, an elevated dinner, and a practise of non-listening.
