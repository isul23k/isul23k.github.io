2026_Sustained Piano_a musical system that expands on the prepared piano and experimental electronic sound practice. It involves the activation of sustained strings through a combination of technical preparations, including computer algorithmic synthesis, transducers, and electromagnetic resonators. The instrument observes acoustic spectra, the resonances inside the architectonic body of the piano, and variable contingencies of materiality. The name Sustained Piano refers both to the sustained tones from the instrument as well as the pianos capacity to remain, move through time, and collaborate with other technologies.

This work developed as a part of a research project that applied Karen Barad's study of diffraction and waveform entanglements that prompted the questions: How electronic music and early music communicate? How a piano participates in networks of electricity? How the technology of art is shaped by social cultural dynamics? and moves towards the blurring of boundaries where the recalibration of sight in Yuk Hui's labors on technodiversity and Mark Fisher's loss of foresight are embeddings of this process.
