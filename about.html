I am a sound-maker and technologist from Aotearoa, New Zealand, living in Berlin, Germany. Since my recent studies at the Institute of Sonology, I have been questioning what listening is and how this shapes our understanding of and approaches to composition and collaboration. My interest over the years have shifted towards investigating the materiality of sound and perceptions thereof - challenging fixed forms of instrumentation and genre classifications to explore and materialise the multi-stabilities that arises with difference.

Some projects characterise my approach to sound. I play an algorithmically-tuned piano in the DIY ensemble latency<3, compose and perform in the experimental opera duo with Viktoria Nikolova, and continue to facilitate radio in unordinary fashion.

Thank you for visiting my page!
