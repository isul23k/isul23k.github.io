hello_
I am a sound-maker and technologist from Aotearoa, New Zealand, currently living in Berlin, Germany.
Since my recent studies at the Institute of Sonology, I have been questioning what listening is
and how this shapes our understandings of and approaches to composition and collaboration.
My interest over the years have shifted towards investigating the materiality of sound and perceptions
thereof - challenging fixed forms of object utility, instrumentation and genre classifications to
explore and materialise the multi-stabilities that comes with difference.

Mostly, I like to relate to contexts of acoustics, technology and site-specific investigations, working
with people, learning by listening, and practicing as essential to process.

Some projects characterise my approach to sound. An experimental opera duo with Viktoria Nikolova,
the electro-acoustic DIY ensemble latency<3, and the continuing facilitations with radio in unordinary
constellations.
