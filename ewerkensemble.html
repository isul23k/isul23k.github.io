The opera performance happened in the Turbine Hall at E-Werk Luckenwalde during a private event, the wedding ceremony of Helen Turner and Pablo Wendel. The hall, former engine room of the power plant, is an incredible acoustic space that produces reverberation around 6 seconds long. To capture the musical performance and sound reflections in the room, I installed a pair of cardiod condensor microphones in ORTF configuration facing the ensemble. This is to enable all four instrumentals to be captured within its field. Additionally there were two omnidirectional microphones suspended at a height half way to the ceiling at opposing sides of the hall.
The four audio channels were mixed and mastered using Reaper and iZotope plugins.
